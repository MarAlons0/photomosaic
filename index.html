Photomosaic

A tribute to the Leopard “Mosaic” screen saver: each photo dissolves into a mosaic of your other photos, and the camera dives into one of them, forever.

Or drop photos / a folder anywhere. A few hundred photos works best.
Keys: F fullscreen · Space pause · N next · S settings

Settings

Zoom out: each photo shrinks into a tile of the next. Applies from the next photo.
More tiles = clearer mosaic and a softer reveal (tiles start smaller). Leopard’s default was 60.
Lays the big photo faintly over the tiles so it reads more clearly.
How far apart copies of the same photo must be.
